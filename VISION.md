# TradeFlow Vision

## Who This Is For

Solo operators and small teams running import/export businesses in a 
fragmenting world. The kind of people who:

- Operate across borders (India ↔ Ireland, US ↔ LatAm, etc.)
- Don't have an IT team or want one
- Care about data sovereignty (don't want their business data on US-only clouds)
- Need multi-currency, multi-jurisdiction from day one
- Move fast, change directions, can't wait for enterprise IT rollouts
- Find SAP/NetSuite/Oracle too expensive, too rigid, too slow

## What Makes TradeFlow Different

Not a smaller SAP. A different category entirely:

- **Sovereignty-first**: self-hostable, multi-region by default, no single-cloud lock-in
- **Multi-region native**: not bolted on. Currencies, regulations, timezones treated as first-class
- **AI-native workflows**: not "AI features" but AI woven into how work happens
- **Solo-friendly pricing**: priced for one operator, not 500-seat licenses
- **Open and modular**: own your data, export anything, integrate anywhere

## Where TradeFlow Goes (As It Earns The Right To Grow)

Stage 1 (now → 6 months): Excellent at import/export ops. Auth, multi-tenant, 
real Postgres backend, browser-verified.

Stage 2 (6-12 months): CRM-grade outreach intelligence (deals, pipeline, 
lead scoring) — natural extension of existing outreach module.

Stage 3 (12-18 months): SCM-grade supplier intelligence (scorecards, 
reorder automation, demand forecasting) — natural extension of inventory.

Stage 4 (18-24 months): BI dashboards across everything (trends, predictions, 
anomalies) — natural extension of all collected data.

Beyond: Pick a direction based on real user feedback. Stay focused.

## Why Now

The world is genuinely re-organizing:
- Supply chains fragmenting (US/China decoupling, friend-shoring, India rising)
- Currency volatility (CBDCs, dollar dominance questioned)
- Trade routes shifting (Red Sea, Suez alternatives)
- Sanctions complexity (50+ overlapping regimes)
- Regulation explosion (DPDPA, GDPR, EU AI Act, CBAM)
- Trust collapse (people seeking alternatives to legacy institutions)

Big SaaS can't authentically serve solo operators in this fragmented world. 
Their pricing assumes Fortune 500 budgets, their compliance assumes US/EU 
operations, their philosophy is "trust us, we're big."

TradeFlow is built for the opposite philosophy.

## Operating Principles

1. Build for today's customer (yourself first) in a way that scales to 
   tomorrow's market.
2. Each new feature must answer "does this serve the VISION?" If yes, do it. 
   If no, defer.
3. Predicting the future is impossible. Thinking clearly about the present 
   is everything.
4. Don't get seduced by "build everything." Pick the right next thing.
